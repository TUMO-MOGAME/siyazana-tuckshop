What happens when you order

When you tap Send order on WhatsApp, the website writes your order message on your own phone and opens WhatsApp. The website does not see or keep the message. If you send it, the shop receives your order, your WhatsApp name and number, and the name or note you chose to add.

The shop uses these only to prepare your order and reply to you, and deletes old order chats [TO CONFIRM: for example, after 30 days].

Services this website uses

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States) hosts the website. Like every web host, its servers record technical details such as your IP address for security. That information is held outside South Africa by GitHub.
  • WhatsApp (WhatsApp LLC / Meta, outside South Africa) carries your order message once you send it, under WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

The website does not use Google Analytics, advertising, or fonts or scripts from other websites.

Your rights

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) you can ask the shop what information it holds about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and object to it being used. Ask the shop on WhatsApp.

If you are not happy with the answer, you can complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa): inforegulator.org.za.
